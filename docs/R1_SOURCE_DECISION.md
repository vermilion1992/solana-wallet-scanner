# R1 source and delivery decision

Decision date: 3 October 2026. Original wallet-wide scope remains the product goal. **B1 has a concrete dependency/source decision; B2 implementation and B3 complete real acceptance remain OPEN.** The authorised cache supports selected-record research. It does not support a completed wallet financial pipeline, and no new provider collection or credentials were used for this decision.

## Inventory of authorised archives

`evidence/REAL_ARCHIVE_INVENTORY.json` inventories the isolated prior QA copy, without testing the user's live database or copying credentials/configuration/ledger. Five real wallet identities have **94 distinct selected raw records in total**. Some saved reports/rebuilds share those inputs; they are not additional transactions. Raw time bounds, selected paid-fee sums and report/frozen-input identities are recorded per anonymised wallet identifier.

The cached 23-record case supports **0.000240394 SOL** in observed network fees and zero known closed named-account episodes. Expected selected fees were separately calculated by direct integer addition of the raw payer-matched `meta.fee`, without calling the production metric functions. That is a bounded arithmetic oracle, not independent chain authentication. Archived current-owned-account lists, balances, pages, blocks and hash links establish their stated collector trust boundary. No archive establishes an exhaustive historical ownership/lifecycle witness, all relevant acquisitions, a completed eligible population or historical boundary valuation. Existing reviewed mainnet buy/sell fixtures prove narrow instruction/consideration observations, not a complete real wallet.

## Primary source feasibility checks

Raw public documents and pinned parser sources are archived in `evidence/references/`; URLs, retrieval hashes and exact excerpts are retained. These documentation requests used no provider key, credits or subscription. Initial obsolete guide URLs returned 404; the canonical indexed-history reference was found through the provider's own navigation, not retried as an environment failure.

The indexed-history documentation improves source feasibility but does not answer the PDF page 6 hidden closed-account counterexample. A complete indexed address transaction set is only the predicate the source actually documents. Authority changes, multiple token programs, transient accounts, historical ownership intervals and acquisition origins need their own supported coverage. A current list or fixed-point scan can miss an entirely hidden round trip with zero residual mismatch. Page 18's source-sufficiency decision therefore remains active.

## Data contract required before the wallet slice

1. Freeze wallet/network, finalized slot and exact reporting, independent 28-day and 90-day windows. Establish a documented, bounded historical-owner/lifecycle population witness, including closed/reassigned accounts and both token programs, independently of a current list/fixed-point search. Retain provenance, coverage exclusions and source limits.
2. Retain all successful and failed raw transactions for that population; page to supported terminal bounds without repeated cursors, missing signatures or silent format/route exclusion. Reconcile snapshot/block/order facts and unknowns. Unsupported formats stay explicit gaps.
3. Reconstruct event-time ownership, aggregate mint quantities and lifecycle boundaries; resolve pre-window acquisitions and all incoming origins. Unknown basis remains unknown. Separate trades, transfers, rent, tips, network/venue costs and valued external flows; allocate an actual fee once.
4. Provide reviewable time-relevant eligible-asset classification. Complete mint aggregate strict-zero positions, preserving losses, breakeven and unresolved eligible cases. Derive realised metrics per dependency; derive economic metrics only with boundary inventories/marks/flows. Keep the 28-day calculation separate from the display window.
5. Archive one genuine wallet/window corpus with independently worked integer/decimal expected results and source-removal, missing-record and unknown-basis controls. A loss or policy miss is acceptable; a profitable MATCH is not required.

## Product decision and stopping point

Retain the original complete-wallet goal. Under current authorisation, the bounded alternative is **sampled, source-linked research of supplied/collected records**: observed fees, supported individual swaps, named-account evidence where available, current token controls and explicit unknowns. It has a separate scope and cannot satisfy the wallet-wide profit/median/count filters or copying safety.

Gate A can accept a stabilised implementation of that existing declared subset. Full R1 is blocked by DATA-01 (unsupported historical-owner/population and complete acceptance inputs), CAP-01 (unimplemented completed-wallet branch), and historical classification/cost/valuation dependencies above. The next source acquisition would require a concrete bounded plan, method access/tariffs and explicit permission to use credentials/credits. This task grants documentation retrieval and offline work, so those acquisitions have not occurred. No subscription purchase, quota reset, threshold relaxation, trading or source-scope substitution is made.
