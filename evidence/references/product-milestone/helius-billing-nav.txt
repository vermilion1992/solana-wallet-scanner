# Helius Billing, Plans & Pricing

Complete guide to Helius plans, pricing, credits, rate limits, autoscaling, and payment options.

## Quick Reference

- Dashboard: https://dashboard.helius.dev/billing
- Free tier: 1M credits/month, no card required
- Paid plans: Start at $49/month (Developer)
- Enterprise plans: Custom pricing
- Additional credits: $5 per million
- Payment methods: Credit card, USDC on Solana

## Credits System

Credits are the unit of billing for RPCs, APIs, and streaming data. Every plan includes monthly credits that reset each billing cycle.

### Credit Priority Order
1. Monthly allocation (resets monthly)
2. Prepaid credits (never expire)
3. Autoscaling credits ($5/M)

### Standard RPC Credits

| Service | Credits | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Standard RPC calls | 1 | Most RPC methods |
| getProgramAccountsV2 | 1 | Paginated version |
| simulateBundle | 1 | Jito bundle simulation |
| Priority Fee API | 1 | Fee estimation |
| getProgramAccounts | 10 | Get all program accounts |
| DAS API (all methods) | 10 | Digital assets |
| Enhanced Transactions | 100 | Parsed transactions |

### Historical Data Credits

| Method | Credits | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| getBlock | 1 | Block data and transactions |
| getBlocks | 1 | List of blocks |
| getBlocksWithLimit | 1 | Blocks with limit |
| getSignaturesForAddress | 1 | Transaction signatures |

| getBlockTime | 1 | Block timestamp |
| getInflationReward | 1 | Inflation rewards |
| getSignatureStatuses | 1 | Transaction signature statuses |
| getTransactionsForAddress | 10+ | Enhanced history (Developer+ only). Full transactions cost 10 credits per 100 returned; signatures-only responses cost 10 credits flat. |
| getTransfersByAddress | 10 | Parsed token and native SOL transfer history (Developer+ only) |

## Autoscaling

Automatically purchase additional credits when monthly allocation is exhausted.

### How It Works

1. Enable autoscaling on your Billing page
2. When credits run out, system auto-purchases more
3. Additional credits: $5 per million
4. Email notifications sent

### Setup Steps

1. Go to your [Billing](https://dashboard.helius.dev/billing) page
2. Find the Autoscaling section
3. Toggle autoscaling on

### Autoscaling Limits

- **Developer & Business**: Capped to avoid unexpected charges
- **Professional**: Unlimited autoscaling
- **Enterprise**: Custom configuration

### Payment Options

**Card Payments:**
- Autoscaling charges payment method automatically

**Crypto Payments:**
- Some users: Autoscaling available
- Others: Purchase prepaid credits manually

Prepaid credits:
- Never expire
- Purchased via dashboard
- Paid in USDC on Solana

### Tracking Usage

- **Email Alerts**: Notifications at credit limits
- **Dashboard**: [Usage tracking](https://dashboard.helius.dev/)

## Paying with Crypto

Pay for Helius plans using USDC on Solana.

### How Crypto Payments Work

1. Navigate to [Billing Page](https://dashboard.helius.dev/billing)
2. Select plan (monthly or annual)
3. Click **Pay with Crypto**
4. Enter billing information
5. Connect wallet or scan QR code
6. Complete payment in USDC
7. Wait for on-chain confirmation

### Key Details

- **Payment Links**: Sent by email and dashboard when due
- **Currency**: USDC on Solana only
- **Confirmation**: Up to 1 hour for dashboard update
- **Cancellation**: Cancel anytime from billing page

### Switching Payment Methods

Contact support to switch between fiat and crypto: https://dashboard.helius.dev/support

## Common Questions

**Q: What happens when I run out of credits?**
A: With autoscaling enabled, credits auto-purchase. Without it, API requests fail until next billing cycle or manual credit purchase.

**Q: Do credits roll over?**
A: Monthly credits reset each billing cycle. Prepaid credits never expire.

**Q: Can I upgrade mid-month?**
A: Yes. You'll be prorated and receive new credit allocation immediately.

**Q: How do I monitor credit usage?**
A: Dashboard shows real-time usage: https://dashboard.helius.dev/

**Q: What's the difference between RPC and DAS rate limits?**
A: RPC limits apply to standard Solana RPC calls. DAS limits apply to Digital Asset Standard API and other enhanced APIs.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Contact support for refund requests: https://dashboard.helius.dev/support
