# Blueprint alignment and remaining evidence gaps

This traces the supplied **Solana Wallet Scanner Free Local Blueprint v0.3** to the current implementation. Page numbers refer to the PDF's 21 physical pages. Numeric threshold fidelity does not prove complete live evidence or a profitable strategy. The previously sampled five live wallet reports remain `UNRESOLVED`, with zero financially qualified wallets.

## Literal page 9 starting preset

Every numeric threshold in `scanner/config.py` matches the PDF page 9 strict starting preset. The populations and evidence prerequisites matter as much as the values.

| PDF page 9 rule | Strict preset / interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting / verification window | 30 / 90 days; recover older purchase costs when needed |
| Net realised meme-trading profit | ≥5 SOL after identifiable costs |
| Realised ROI on disposed acquisition cost | ≥10% |
| Median completed-position ROI | ≥5% |
| Completed-position win rate | 50–85%; breakeven episodes remain in the denominator |
| Median strict completed hold | 1–72 hours, from first economic acquisition to final strict-zero exit |
| Completed positions | ≥50 in 30 days and ≥100 in 90 days |
| Traded qualifying meme mints | 20–100, excluding settlement assets and spam |
| First positive sale within five minutes | ≤10% of completed positions |
| Mean buys / sells per completed episode | 1–2 / 1–3 |
| Positive weeks | ≥3 of four consecutive seven-day periods over `[report end − 28 days, report end)`; independent of a custom shorter report window |
| Largest positive mint contribution | ≤25% of positive net realised period profit |
| Economic P&L | Positive, with boundary equity and external flows reconciled |
| Evidence gates | History, identity, basis, positions, fees, classification, valuation and findings review |

Presets are editable as the blueprint requests. An edited preset is not the literal page 9 starting preset. A known threshold miss remains a policy miss; trading 101 qualifying mints does not imply fraud. Missing data, open holdings, losing positions, unsupported routes and costs cannot disappear to create winners.

## Method changes and saved reports

Page 3 states: “Accounting methodology is a separate, versioned expert setting: changing dust or cost allocation triggers a rebuild, not a cosmetic filter update.” Page 14 distinguishes versioned runtime profile, provider-cost manifest, accounting methodology and preset, and separates **re-filter**, **rebuild** and **re-fetch**. Page 7 preserves original observations so a parser correction can produce a traceable new report.

The earlier fee correction created `fifo-v2` accounting results and a separately versioned conditional research definition. The independent-review revision creates `fifo-v3`: four-week consistency always uses `[report end − 28 days, report end)` with its own coverage rather than truncating to a custom shorter report window. Weekly costs and realised slices use that same interval. Missing interval history, basis or fees remains unknown, including when another metric interval is complete. Per-interval and per-metric coverage is visible; declarations from normalized inputs are not raw-provider certificates. Old `fifo-v1`/`fifo-v2` financial snapshots retain their original method and values; preserving their immutability is the implementation's provenance choice, rather than an explicit quoted PDF requirement. A threshold preview or newly attached cached copy-review annotation does not apply the current accounting method to an old financial result. That requires a new rebuild from its evidence.

The PDF does not literally promise detection of “bait” wallets or followers. The added copy review addresses the user's concern through evidence-linked behavior observations and explicit unresolved review fields. Selling 90% early while retaining a small remainder can be observed; exploiting followers or planning a rug cannot be inferred from that timing alone. No absence-of-findings result is a legitimacy certificate.
